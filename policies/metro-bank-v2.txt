METRO SMALL FINANCE BANK LIMITED
Credit Policy Extract: Premium Personal Loan (PPL) - Revision 2
Approved by the Credit Committee of the Board, meeting dated 18 September 2026
This extract supersedes the PPL extract dated 12 June 2026 in its entirety.

Section 1: Geography
To support expansion into Tier-2 markets, the PPL programme is now offered in the following cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Indore, Ahmedabad and Jaipur. Applications from any other location shall be declined at the sourcing stage.

Section 2: Bureau requirement
A minimum CIBIL score of 750 is required. No deviations are permitted on this parameter.

Section 3: Income requirement
Minimum net monthly income: Rs. 40,000.

=== PAGE BREAK ===

Section 4: Fixed obligations
Fixed obligations to income ratio (FOIR), considering existing EMIs only, shall not exceed 45%.

Section 5: Loan amount
Minimum loan amount Rs. 1,00,000; maximum loan amount Rs. 25,00,000.

Section 6: Pricing
Interest rates range from 10.75% to 16.50% per annum.

Section 7: Relationship benefits
Customers holding a Metro SFB salary account for 6 months or more are eligible for a rate concession of 0.50%.

Section 8: Tier-2 sourcing
For Indore, Ahmedabad and Jaipur, sourcing is permitted only through bank branches and empanelled DSAs for the first two quarters.
