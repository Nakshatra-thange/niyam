METRO SMALL FINANCE BANK LIMITED
Credit Policy Extract: Premium Personal Loan (PPL)
Approved by the Credit Committee of the Board, meeting dated 12 June 2026

Section 1: Geography
The PPL programme is offered only in the following cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Chennai. Applications from any other location shall be declined at the sourcing stage.

Section 2: Bureau requirement
A minimum CIBIL score of 780 is required. No deviations are permitted on this parameter.

Section 3: Income requirement
Minimum net monthly income: Rs. 50,000.

=== PAGE BREAK ===

Section 4: Fixed obligations
Fixed obligations to income ratio (FOIR), considering existing EMIs only, shall not exceed 40%.

Section 5: Loan amount
Minimum loan amount Rs. 1,00,000; maximum loan amount Rs. 25,00,000.

Section 6: Pricing
Interest rates range from 10.50% to 16.00% per annum.

Section 7: Relationship benefits
Customers holding a Metro SFB salary account for 6 months or more are eligible for a rate concession of 0.50%.
