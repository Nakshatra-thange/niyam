KIRANA CREDIT NBFC LIMITED
Product Note: Dukaan Saathi Business Loan (Daily Repayment)
Document No. KC/PN/DS/v2

About the product
Dukaan Saathi is a short-tenure working capital loan for kirana stores, general stores, medical shops and other small retail outlets. Repayment is collected daily through UPI autopay.

Eligibility at a glance

Parameter            | Norm
---------------------|-----------------------------------------------
Borrower type        | Self-employed individuals and proprietors / business owners only
Business vintage     | Minimum 12 months of continuous operations
Monthly income       | Rs. 15,000 per month or more (from business)
Applicant age        | 21 to 65 years
Loan amount          | Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 3,00,000

=== PAGE BREAK ===

Pricing
Interest is charged at 1.5% to 2.5% per month on a reducing balance basis.

Documentation
- Shop must hold GST registration or an Udyam registration certificate.
- Geo-tagged photo of the shop front is mandatory.

Bureau
- Credit bureau score is not mandatory. New-to-credit shop owners are welcome; where a score is available, it will be used only for pricing.

Exclusions
- Liquor shops, lottery outlets and tobacco wholesalers are not funded.
