DHANREKHA CAPITAL PVT LTD
Internal Circular No. DC/CR/14 - Personal Loan Sourcing Guidelines
To: All Branch Managers and Channel Partners

Team, please note the updated norms below. Kripya sabhi logins inhi norms ke hisaab se karein.

Who can apply:
- Age: 23 se 60 saal tak (both inclusive).
- Salaried as well as self-employed customers are fine, as long as income is documented.

Bureau:
- CIBIL 680+ only. Below 680 = straight reject, no deviation.

Income:
- Minimum take-home of Rs. 20k per month. Bank statement of last 6 months mandatory.

=== PAGE BREAK ===

Existing loans:
- Total existing EMIs should not be more than 60% of monthly income.

Ticket size:
- Max loan amount Rs. 8 lakh. No minimum ticket.

Pricing:
- ROI between 14% and 24% p.a. depending on profile.

Other notes:
- For applicants above 55 years of age, a co-applicant is mandatory.
- Rented-accommodation customers need 1 year at current address.

Regards,
Credit Team, Dhanrekha Capital
